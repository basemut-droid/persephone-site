## 1. CRITICAL — the Angebote dropdown cannot be used with a mouse

The structure is right — a real `<a href="/angebote/">` plus a separate toggle button
with `aria-expanded`. That part stays.

**The bug:** the submenu opens on hover, but the hover region is only the word
"Angebote" itself. Moving the pointer down toward the submenu leaves that region, the
menu closes, and the three subpages become physically unreachable by mouse. Reported by
the site owner, who could not navigate to any of them.

Fix all three causes:

- **Scope the open state to the whole `<li class="nav-dropdown">`**, which contains both
  the trigger and the menu — not to the link or `.nav-dropdown-trigger` alone. Use
  `:hover` **and** `:focus-within` on that element.
- **Remove the dead gap** between trigger and menu. If the menu needs visual separation,
  achieve it with `padding-top` on the menu itself (so the gap is still inside the
  hoverable area) rather than `margin-top`, or bridge it with a transparent
  `::before`. A pointer must never cross unhoverable space to reach the menu.
- **Add a short close delay**, roughly 150–200 ms, so a diagonal pointer path doesn't
  close it.

The toggle button must keep working independently for click, touch and keyboard — that
is the path for anyone who does not hover.

**Verify by actually doing it**, not by reading the CSS: drive a real pointer from
"Angebote" to "Selbsthilfegruppe" in a browser and confirm the menu stays open the whole
way. Then confirm keyboard access (Tab to the toggle, Enter, Tab through the items) and
that the menu still works at 390px.

### 1b. Spacing between "Angebote" and "Blog" is too wide

The caret toggle button adds width, so the gap after "Angebote" is visibly larger than
between the other items. Tighten it so the nav reads evenly, while keeping the toggle's
touch target at least 44px — use negative margin or absolute positioning rather than
shrinking the hit area.
